CURRENT AVAILABILITY

Private use. Deliberate validation.

The analyst tools are currently validated with synthetic demonstration data. Broker connectivity is under validation, beginning with a controlled Alpaca Paper OAuth workflow. Live syncing and tax reconstruction from real brokerage records are not presented as production-validated capabilities.

Where an operator separately authorizes a supported connection, the application is designed to retrieve account information, positions and available activity records through read-only requests. It does not request trading or account-write OAuth scopes.

Tax Sentinel is not a trading service. It cannot place, modify or cancel orders, transfer funds, or manage a brokerage account. This public homepage offers no account connection, login, application download or financial-data upload.

LIMITS THAT STAY VISIBLE

Evidence before conclusions.

Missing activity, unknown acquisition basis, incomplete account history and open wash-sale windows remain explicit. No detected conflict is not a definitive tax clearance. Paper-account records are simulations, not actual taxable investment history.

Tax Sentinel supports analysis, not tax-return filing or personalized tax, legal or investment advice. Review conclusions with a qualified professional when appropriate.
